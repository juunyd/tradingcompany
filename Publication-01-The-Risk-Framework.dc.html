The problem

A strategy can survive a losing trade.
Poor risk management often cannot.

Most traders spend their attention on entries. The account, however, is decided by what happens when an idea is wrong — how much was at stake, whether a limit existed, and whether it was honoured.

Risk is one of the few variables a trader fully controls. Left undefined, it gets decided in the moment, under pressure, by the position itself.

What this publication helps you understand

What you are actually risking

How to express exposure as a number you set in advance, rather than a feeling you assess after the fact.

Where accounts break

Why a sequence of ordinary losses does more damage than a single bad trade, and what that implies for limits.

How to set a boundary you will keep

The difference between a rule you wrote down and a rule that survives a difficult session.
