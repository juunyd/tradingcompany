The problem

Most advice on quitting
already has an answer in mind.

Some voices say never give up. Others say trading is a losing game for almost everyone. Neither is looking at your situation.

This book gives you a structured way to answer the question yourself — from your own record and your own circumstances, not from someone else’s conviction.

What this book helps you understand

What your record shows

How to read your own trading history for what it shows, rather than what you hope it shows.

What trading is costing you

How to weigh the full cost — money, time and attention — not just the P&L.

What each answer means

What stopping, pausing or continuing would actually involve, stated plainly.

Who this is for

  • Traders asking whether to continue after a hard stretch
