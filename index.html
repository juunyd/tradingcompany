Editorial note

Trading is full of information.

But useful information is different.

Trading Company publishes for one moment in particular: the stretch after a loss, when there is plenty of advice and very little of it useful.

We write for traders who prefer structure over reassurance, and an honest question over an easy answer. No shortcuts, no guarantees — and no assumption that you should keep trading.

How the series is written

01

Written for after

Not for the trade you are about to take — for the weeks after one went badly.

02

Read in order

03

Structure, not comfort

No motivation, no reassurance you have not earned. Questions you answer for yourself.

04

Nothing here tells you what to trade, and nothing here promises the money back.
