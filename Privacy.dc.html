Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

01

Who we are

Trading Company (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates tradingcompany.in and sells digital publications. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

02

Information we collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Name, email address, and phone number (if provided) when you make a purchase
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Razorpay, our payment gateway. We do not collect, see, or store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers.
  • Any information you send us when contacting support

Information collected automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, device type, and general location (city/region level)
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, and referring website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 3)

03

Cookies and tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site and understand how it’s used:

  • Essential cookies — required for checkout and site functionality to work.
  • Meta Pixel — we use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who may be interested in our publications. The Meta Pixel collects information such as pages you visit, purchases made, and general device/browser information, and shares this with Meta in accordance with Meta’s own privacy policy. This helps us understand which ads led to a purchase and to build audiences of people with similar interests for future advertising. You can control how Meta uses this data through your Meta Ad Preferences settings.
  • Analytics — we use website analytics tools to understand traffic patterns, popular content, and site performance. This data is aggregated and used to improve the site; it is not used to individually identify you.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect checkout functionality.

04

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and deliver your purchase
  • Send order confirmations and, where necessary, respond to support requests
  • Measure and improve our advertising, including through Meta Pixel
  • Understand site usage and improve the user experience
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations

05

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate the business, specifically:

  • Razorpay — payment processing
  • Supabase — secure storage of order records and delivery of digital files
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and content delivery
  • Resend — sending order confirmation emails
  • Meta — advertising measurement and audience building, via the Meta Pixel

Each of these providers processes data under their own privacy and security policies. We do not share your information with any other third party except where required by law.

06

Data retention

We retain order and transaction records for as long as necessary to comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations, and to resolve any disputes. You may request deletion of your personal data subject to these legal retention requirements (see Section 7).

07

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing (such as Meta Pixel tracking, via your browser or Meta’s ad preference tools)
  • Raise a grievance regarding how your data is handled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@tradingcompany.in.

08

Data security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access-restricted databases, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09

Children’s privacy

Our publications and services are not directed at, or intended for use by, individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10

International data transfer

Some of our service providers (such as Meta) may process data outside India. By using our site, you consent to this transfer, which is carried out in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

12

Contact us

For any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at support@tradingcompany.in.
